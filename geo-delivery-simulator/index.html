Setup data

My location

Sets MY_LOCATION and flies the map there - leave blank to keep the current label.

Known stores (JSON array)

Paste an array of {name, lat, lng, radiusKm} objects (same shape shown here, pre-filled with the current KNOWN_STORES) and click Load - replaces every store pin, delivery-radius circle, and candidate list used by the panels below, immediately.

1 · Delivery zones

Click "Draw a delivery zone", then click points on the map to trace a polygon and double-click to finish. You'll be asked to name it - that's one GeoBoundary (ownerType=RESTAURANT). Zone centroids also stand in as "restaurant locations" for panels 4 and 5 below when the live backend is off.

2 · GeoHash grid

Overlays the GeoHash cells covering the current map view, at the chosen precision - exactly what GeoHash.encode(lat, lng, precision) buckets a point into.

3 · Simulate a user search

Runs the exact pipeline from InMemoryPolygonBoundaryService.ownersContaining(...): GeoHash prefilter, nearest-N narrowing (a sorted-distance stand-in for the real KD-Tree - behaviorally identical on a handful of demo points), then containment on the survivors - point-in-polygon for drawn zones, isWithinRadius for each KNOWN_STORES pin's own delivery radius (always active, no drawing needed). Result appears in the panel at the bottom of the screen.

4 · Nearby restaurants

Ranks the KNOWN_STORES pins (plus any drawn zone centroids) by distance from the point you click - exactly DistanceCalculator.topNNearest plus distanceMatrix; ETA uses the same naive 25 km/h etaMinutes default. Works immediately, no drawing needed - or turn on "Live backend" to use its own dummy restaurants instead.

5 · Route simulator

Compares a straight-line reference (and, with the live backend on, the real RoutingEngine) against real road routes and alternatives from OSRM's public routing API. Only the shortest road route is drawn on the map by default - click "Show all routes" to draw every alternative too (all are always listed in the results table below either way).

Known stores

Store pins are always shown; their dashed delivery-radius circles (KNOWN_STORES[].radiusKm, editable per store) are off by default to keep the map uncluttered - toggle them on here when you actually want to see coverage.
